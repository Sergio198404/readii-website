The Platform平台功能

One platform.
Built on our AI voice engine.一个平台，
构建于AI 语音引擎之上。

Readii is an AI voice technology platform for British English. Our proprietary engine scores pronunciation in real time, identifies phonetic gaps, and delivers personalised feedback across 1,000+ audio lessons recorded by native British teachers. Built for non-native speakers from age 3 upwards.Readii 是面向英式英语的 AI 语音技术平台。我们的自研引擎实时打分、识别发音弱点，在 1000 多节由英国本土教师录制的音频课程中提供个性化反馈。专为 3 岁以上的非母语学习者打造。

Beyond the platform平台之外

For users who progress through our pronunciation training, Readii offers value-added services that complement their journey in the UK — including UK cultural programmes, professional communication coaching, and partner introductions for cross-border learners.对于完成发音训练的用户，Readii 提供配套的增值服务以辅助他们的英国之旅——包括英国文化课程、职业沟通辅导，以及面向跨境学习者的合作伙伴对接。

Xiaoyu (Kevin) Su
Founder & CEO · UK Innovator Founder创始人兼CEO · 英国创新创业者

Kevin brings 10+ years of experience in EdTech and internet technology, having worked at UMU (enterprise learning platform, Beijing) and ChinaHR. He moved to Canterbury, England in 2023 to build Readii — an AI-powered platform bridging the communication and cultural gap between China and the UK.

Originally designed around British teachers delivering live reading sessions, Readii evolved into a fully AI-powered platform as voice recognition and pronunciation assessment technology matured. This transition — replacing ongoing teacher delivery with scalable AI — is at the heart of what Readii does: using technology to make high-quality British English accessible to any child, anywhere, without the cost and constraints of human-staffed delivery.Kevin 拥有超过10年教育科技与互联网行业经验，曾就职于 UMU（企业学习平台，北京）与中华英才网。2023年移居英国坎特伯雷，创立 Readii——弥合中英沟通与文化差距的 AI 技术平台。

Readii 最初设计围绕英国外教进行实时阅读教学，随着语音识别与发音评估技术的成熟，平台完全转型为 AI 驱动模式。这一转变——用可扩展的 AI 替代持续的人工教学交付——正是 Readii 的核心创新：让高质量的英式英语教育不再受限于人力成本，惠及每一个孩子。
